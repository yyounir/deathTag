# DEATHTag

## About/Story
DEATHTag is a maze game where you guide lil' Youn, a hobo, to safely escape from the Bronx by using the New York City Subway! Violetta, his childhood enemy, is out to chase after him wherever he goes, and it is your job to guide him to safety (and also to reduce him from being hungry)! Eat food scattered in the maze to increase your speed and tackle eating food in hard-to-reach areas to compete for a high score!

## Structure
<ul>
<li>Used several array implementations of a stack to determine the position of the walls, sprites, food, and obstacles that are scattered in this maze</li>
<li>Implmented chasing algorithms for the enemy character to chase after the the player based on their position</li>
<li>Optimized memory management by utilizing efficient data structures (2D Arrays) for map generation, ensuring smooth 60 FPS performance during active entity tracking.</li>
</ul>
